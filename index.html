Import a bank statement

close

Works with a statement downloaded before the month ends. The file is read on your device and is not uploaded; only the lines you choose to import are saved. New lines are added, lines already in your list are left alone, and lines whose amount disagrees are shown for you to decide.

Miles on imported lines are estimates from the card's rules and the merchant name. Edit a line afterwards if the bank credited differently.
