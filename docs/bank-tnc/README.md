Most of these are rewards-programme terms: they give earn rules and exclusions, not FX fees. The FX fee for a bank normally sits in its cardmember agreement or fee schedule, which is not saved here yet for UOB, OCBC, HSBC or Maybank.

Maybank important notice (24 Sep 2021), https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/about_us/important-notices/2021/change-of-credit-card-foreign-currency-transaction-administrative-fee.page : bank admin fee 1.75% -> 2.25% from 1 Nov 2021, plus 1% card association conversion charge, for all foreign-currency card transactions.
HSBC credit cards FAQ (undated, read 29 Oct 2026), https://www.hsbc.com.sg/credit-cards/faq/ : foreign-currency transaction fee 2.25% bank + 1% card association = 3.25%; 1% DCC fee, including SGD transactions processed overseas.
